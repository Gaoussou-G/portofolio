Concept proposal

After-hours card pickup at the ATM

An idea based on what I see in the field. It is not an NCR product or initiative, and it uses no internal information.

The problem

I have seen what happens when someone loses their card after the branch has closed. Blocking it takes seconds in the banking app, and so does requesting a new one. But the replacement is not usable until the next day, and on a weekend the card may not arrive until Monday. Until then the customer cannot pay for anything, while the nearest ATM sits open all night and takes no part in the solution.

The proposal

  1. The customer uploads identity documents in the banking app.
  2. Customer service reviews them and confirms the request.
  3. The customer receives a one-time code.
  4. At the ATM, the customer enters the code and collects the card.

Identity is checked by people before the customer reaches the machine, so the ATM never decides on its own who gets a card. It trades some speed for a lower fraud risk.

Risks I would address

  • Stolen or forged documentsHuman review and a selfie match against the ID.
  • Intercepted codeShort expiry, single use.
  • Card never collectedA time limit, then it is retained and destroyed or returned.
  • ActivationThe card works only once collected, with the customer choosing a PIN.
  • HardwareBlank-card stock, jams and refills, where field experience matters.
